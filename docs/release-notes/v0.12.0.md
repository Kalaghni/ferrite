# FERRITE v0.12.0

## Underground

Quarry's mine and Ridgeline's cave are real tunnels now, not covered passages on the surface.

- **Quarry:** an adit opens from the first terrace into the face of the second. Inside, a timbered drift with rails and lamps runs round under the second terrace and right beneath the headframe's shaft, then climbs a long incline back to daylight beside it.
- **Ridgeline:** a damp, mossy cave bends through the ridge between the saddle and the southern pass, under four to seven metres of rock, with a couple of lanterns left behind.

Rock walls and roofs stop players, jumps, bullets and sight lines; the hill above is ordinary ground. Both tunnels are mirrored for the other team, and bots follow them. See the [expanded map guide](../maps/landmarks.md#underground-tunnels).

## The Fallen Giant, crashed

The aircraft on Ridgeline now looks like it came down hard: the fuselage is broken in three and twisted, skin is torn and peeled back to the frames, engines are burnt out or gone, the fin leans with a torn tip, and the hull lies in a furrow of earth, scorch marks, debris and snapped trees.

It also plays better:

- buckled frames inside the hold break the 84-metre sightline that ran from end to end through C;
- pallets give C cover, and fallen roof skin lies under each tear;
- the landing gear, engine and container you can see outside are solid, and the port wing folds up out of reach - nothing at walking height is see-through any more;
- the hold has a metal deck, and the ground round the wreck blends into the ridge instead of a flat mesa.

See the [Fallen Giant guide](../maps/fallen-giant.md).

## Monuments

Three giant set pieces, all scenery beyond reach:

- **Foundry Valley - the Broken Viaduct:** a railway bridge spans the valley high over C, one span collapsed, a freight locomotive hanging nose-down over the gap. It sways in the wind and a signal lamp blinks.
- **Quarry - the Titan Excavator:** a walking dragline on the south ridge with a boom reaching over the pit and a bucket big enough to swallow a truck. Its house slews now and then, the bucket swings and the ropes groan.
- **Spillway - the Great Dam:** a concrete dam towers beyond the north fence, water weeping through its floodgates, a maintenance gantry crawling along the crest. Every so often its sirens are tested.

## Weapon finishes

Every finish now has its own pattern, material and detailing, on every gun: Oxide's copper and verdigris, Arctic's digital camo, Woodland blots, Midnight shards, Hazard chevrons, Sandstorm tiger stripes, Emberfall's glowing cracks, Cobalt Steel's Damascus ripples, Carbon weave with red hardware, Toxic's glowing honeycomb, Rose Gold with a pearl inlay, Glacier facets, and Gilded's gold scroll on black. Names, prices and what you own are unchanged. Guns more than 10 m away skip the pattern, so a full server draws fewer triangles than before.

## Cinematic camera

- **P** flies a free camera (WASD, Space/Ctrl for up and down, Shift faster, Alt slower), **F1** hides the HUD, **F12** saves a screenshot to `screenshots/` beside the game.
- In the camera: mouse wheel changes the field of view, **B** turns on depth of field with autofocus (Z/X to focus by hand, [ and ] for the aperture), **K** holds the action, and 1-4 set the screenshot to up to four times screen size.
- All keys can be changed in SETTINGS > CONTROLS > CAMERA.

The launcher and lobby backgrounds are now shots of Slag Cathedral staged in the game with this camera; the commands that made them are in `docs/keyart/`.

## Safer online play

- **Session handshake (protocol 19).** A server answers a first hello with a small challenge and only gives a slot to a client that answers it, so spoofed addresses cannot take slots or have traffic reflected at them. Each session gets a random token that every later packet must carry, so nobody else on the Internet can send commands, chat or disconnects for you.
- **Bounded work.** The server processes a limited number of packets per tick and per address, allows at most 8 sessions per IP address, and keeps only the newest 256 kill-feed entries.
- **In step.** The volcano, machinery, wind and beacons now run on the server's clock, so everyone sees an eruption at the same moment, whenever they joined.

Traffic is still not encrypted. Read [SECURITY.md](../../SECURITY.md) before hosting on the Internet.

## Safer updates

From this version on, the launcher's updater:

- downloads only from this project's GitHub releases over HTTPS, and refuses a package whose SHA-256 does not match the release's `SHA256SUMS` file;
- keeps the previous version, and if a new version fails to start, puts the previous one back by itself the next time you launch;
- offers SETTINGS > VIDEO > ROLL BACK (or `ferrite --rollback`) to go back by hand.

The update to v0.12.0 itself is still installed by v0.11's updater; the checks apply to updates after it.

## For contributors

- CI now checks formatting, `clippy -D warnings` and `cargo deny` (advisories, licences, sources), and publishes a release only after every platform builds, with a `SHA256SUMS` file.
- `cargo run --release -p ferrite --example triangle_profile` prints the triangle count of every map by part.

## Compatibility

The network protocol changed (19), so everyone in a match needs v0.12.0, including the dedicated server. Older clients are told the server speaks a newer protocol.
