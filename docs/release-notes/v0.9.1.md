# FERRITE v0.9.1

## Music

- Added "Barbed Wire" by breadKernel to the song rotation. It plays on the launcher, and during matches when music during matches is switched on in settings. The lobby and Slag Cathedral keep their own tracks.

The network protocol is unchanged, so v0.9.0 and v0.9.1 players can share a match and the dedicated server does not need updating. Players on v0.9.0 can use **UPDATE & RESTART** in the launcher.
