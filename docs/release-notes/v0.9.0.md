# FERRITE v0.9.0

## Slag Cathedral

- Added Slag Cathedral, the seventh battlefield: a bell foundry built on the scale of a cathedral, with a crucible nave, twin furnace towers, walkable side galleries, covered service passages and its own score.
- Two open lava channels run the length of the casting floor. Standing in the melt burns 40 health a second for either team, and a death there is reported as `Lava`. Each channel has three grated bridges and a running jump clears it. Bots cross on the bridges and will not strafe into the lava.
- Two bell yards fill the map's quiet corners with a landmark and hard cover: bell No. 6, cracked and shored in timber, and No. 7, still in its mould.
- The works is dressed as a place that still runs with nobody on shift: lavafalls from the furnace launders, a steam organ, leaded glass and shafts of coloured light, a ladle railway from gate to gate, flying buttresses, lit control cabins, a smoke-reddened sky, and small things to find. See the [map guide](../maps/slag-cathedral.md).

## Relay

- The four antenna masts are rebuilt as towers you can climb. A steel stair wraps each tower's service core and ends on a railed deck: 6 m up on the array towers by B and D, 9 m up on the exchange towers either side of C. Steps are walked, not jumped.
- Decks have rails on every edge and two plate parapets facing the nearby objective. The stair is exposed all the way up, and there is one way up. Bots stay on the ground but will shoot at a player on a deck.
- A listening dish on a concrete pedestal stands in each half. The pedestals are new cover.
- Relay has its own evening light, sky and far ranges, cabling between the towers, turning radar heads, flag lines, and a station that was left a moment ago. The exchange beacons signal to each other in Morse. See the [map guide](../maps/relay.md).
- Relay's terrain is levelled under the towers and dishes, and its scattered rocks, trees and lane cover have moved slightly as a result.

## Controls

- Added keyboard and mouse rebinding in SETTINGS > CONTROLS, grouped into movement, combat, interface and loadout pages. Bindings are saved, conflicting assignments are rejected, and defaults can be restored. The + button adds alternatives, including Mouse 4 / Mouse 5 side buttons, so Melee can use V and Mouse 5 together.
- Added controller support with mapped FPS controls, hotplug handling, analog movement and aiming, radial stick deadzones, trigger hysteresis and press-to-toggle sprint. Controller settings include sensitivity, deadzone, enable/disable and Y inversion.
- Added a controller cursor for launcher, pause and loadout menus: sticks / D-pad move, A selects and B goes back.
- On-screen key hints reflect remapped actions.

## Fixes

- Weapon finishes no longer stay unchanged until redeploy. Equipped cosmetics update locally at once and replicate to other players; finishes for different pending weapons stay queued for deployment.

## Compatibility

The network protocol changed for analog movement, and two maps have new collision. Everyone in a match needs v0.9.0, including the dedicated server. Players on v0.8.x can use **UPDATE & RESTART** in the launcher.

## Verification

- New tests walk a player up every Relay stair on five seeds without jumping, prove the deck rails hold, and check the towers are mirrored and clear of objectives and spawns.
- Slag Cathedral tests cover the lava spans, bridges, burn damage and kill-feed entry, the bell yards, and all gallery routes.
- Regression coverage was added for binding persistence and conflicts, controller input states, analog speed and prediction, and live finish replication.
- Relay's two entries in the map-geometry regression test were re-captured for the new towers. The other five maps are unchanged.
- Not yet verified: controller behaviour on physical hardware, frame rate on the two redressed maps, and how strong the Relay exchange decks are in a full match.
