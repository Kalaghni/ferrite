# FERRITE v0.8.0

Release-note draft for the next version.

## Maps

- Added **Spillway**, drained waterworks with a sunken channel, pump halls, a raised floodgate and outer service lanes.
- Added **Relay**, a highland communications station with antenna towers, generator shelters and rocky pine flanks.
- Expanded map selection, voting and rotation to six maps. The original four maps retain their terrain, cover and objective layouts.

## Soldiers and weapons

- Enhanced all six soldier looks with launcher-inspired silhouettes, faceted helmets and armor, broad shoulder plates, and team-marked field packs with white recognition bars.
- Improved articulated movement, foot placement, weight shifts, aiming and weapon handling through sprint, crouch, slide, jump, reload and death poses.
- Cleaned up the existing gun models and their actions, including shotguns, the revolver, Deuce and Ranger. First person, other soldiers and loadout previews share the weapon geometry.
- Reworked iron sights, red dots, holographic sights, the 2.5x optic and 4x / 8x scopes with fitted mounts, protected posts, faceted housings, scope rings, turrets and open aiming apertures. Front sights connect to the barrels, and the compact optic's reticle fits its window.

## Weapon finishes

- Added **Oxide**, **Arctic**, **Woodland**, **Midnight**, **Hazard** and **Sandstorm** alongside Factory. Every existing gun supports the new finishes.
- Applied angled bands or digital camouflage directly to weapon surfaces, including moving receivers, furniture and magazines.
- Click the large primary or sidearm model in the loadout panel, or press **7 / 8**, to cycle its finish. Choices are saved per weapon, apply at the next deploy, and appear in first person, on soldiers, in the killcam and in loadout previews. Bots use a mix of finishes.

## Fixes

- Render every page of larger dynamic meshes so detailed squads and equipment remain visible; include all pages in the triangle counter.
- Preserve the local player's weapon finish on their corpse and save finish changes after direct host/join launches.
- Keep all six map names and vote labels within their lobby cards.
- Track remote reload and action phases for weapon and hand animation.

## Verification and review tools

- Added deterministic asset-review galleries for player looks and poses, weapons, finishes, sights and map overviews, without joining a server or changing saved settings.
- Added checks for original-map geometry preservation, new-map symmetry and navigation, soldier poses, weapon actions and model budgets, finish validation and replication, paint bounds, clear sight apertures and dynamic mesh paging.
- Updated the discovery test to find its server while another local game is running.
- Verified **41 tests** in release mode on Windows, bot matches on both new maps, and in-game aiming views for the red dot, holo and compact optic.

The weapon roster, weapon statistics, movement rules and player collision dimensions are unchanged.
