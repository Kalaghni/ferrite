# FERRITE v0.14.0

A new map, Canopy, built round play that exists nowhere else in FERRITE: lines you ride over the fight, cloth that hides without protecting, and a clock that takes that cover away. And a new map picker for hosting and the lobby vote.

## Canopy - the Hollow Crown

A botanical reserve in the roots and lower boughs of a colossal strangler fig, centred on C. Seed collectors once crossed its branches on powered harvesting slings; now those lines carry soldiers over the fight in the hollow under the trunk.

- **The tree.** Four trunks rise round the root well at C. A continuous ring of walkable boughs 18 m up joins them, with branches reaching in over the well that end in a drop to the ground. Two-flight stairs climb from the collector terraces behind B and D, and ladders run up the flanks to hatches in the boughs.
- **The ground still works.** Every objective and both bases can be reached on foot, and nothing up the tree blocks a ground route.
- **The Hollow Crown.** Above anything you can reach, the trunks climb into great limbs under a leaf roof 164 m across, with a well of open sky over C. The trunks are wrapped in fig roots, and the trees round the reserve grow rainforest greens.

See the [Canopy guide](../maps/canopy.md).

## Seedlines

Six powered lines run from the collector courts behind B and D up to the boughs, and in a ring round the tree over them.

- **Riding.** Stand on a turquoise dock and press **E** (the new Use key, rebindable under SETTINGS > CONTROLS) to take the line you are facing. Forward reels you along at up to 14 m/s and back returns at up to 8 m/s. Let go and you slow to a hang; crouch brakes hard, and jump lets go.
- **Fighting.** Taking a line draws your sidearm. You can shoot it, but you cannot swap, reload, throw or melee while you ride.
- Riders hang in a yellow sling under a brass pulley, and you can hear their motor while they move.

## Shade curtains and the crown gust

Two banks of striped shade cloth can be let down across the root well.

- **Cranks.** Hold **E** for just over a second at a bank's crank wheel, on the inward face of its trunk, to let it down or roll it up. A bank then rests for six seconds.
- **Cloth hides, it does not protect.** Players and bots cannot see through it, but bodies, bullets and grenades go straight through.
- **The gust.** Every two minutes a gust comes through the crown. A warning goes up and the cranks lock; six seconds later every curtain furls, whoever wants it down, and the cranks stay locked for another 24 seconds. It never hurts or moves anyone; it takes the cover away on a clock everyone can see.

## Capture the Flag on Canopy

A flag dropped up the tree - on a bough, the stairs or a seedline - falls to the nearest open ground below, so it is never stranded where only a climber can reach it.

## A new map picker

- **HOST & PLAY** lists the maps down the right of the screen beside the live view, with a top-down plan of the map under the pointer (or the chosen one) marked for the chosen mode, and its description. Click a row to choose it, or step through with the arrow keys. A window too narrow for the list keeps the old picker.
- **The lobby vote** is a grid of map cards, each with its plan, name, vote count and a meter of its share of the votes, with the modes in a column beside it. What will be played is marked NEXT, your vote is in your team's colour, and each card has a tip with a larger plan. The arrow keys move your vote.
- Plans are drawn from the map data itself, so every map has one, Canopy included.

## Compatibility

The network protocol changed (21), so everyone in a match needs v0.14.0, including the dedicated server. Older clients are told the server speaks a newer protocol.

Bots play every mode on Canopy but stay on the ground: they do not ride seedlines, work cranks or climb into the tree.
