# FERRITE v0.15.2

The map maker's controls, made easier to find and quicker to use.

## Changes

- **Right mouse works the brush the other way.** In TERRAIN, the left button raises and the right lowers; smooth and roughen (NOISE) are a pair; with FLAT the right button smooths. Shift with the left button does the same, for a trackpad. RAMP has no opposite, so there the right button still looks around.
- **Middle mouse looks around** in every tool. The right button still looks everywhere but TERRAIN.
- **A panel you can click.** The tools, the terrain modes (as chips), the block kind, the grid, the tint and the objective being placed all respond to a click. Brush size and strength have - and + buttons, and the panel says what each mouse button does.
- **Wheel shortcuts for the brush.** Ctrl+wheel sets its size and Alt+wheel its strength, without looking away from the ground. The old keys ([ ] and - =) still work.
- **Drag to move.** With SELECT, press on a block or a tree and drag it over the ground. It moves in whole grid steps and keeps its footing; the mirrored twin can be dragged too. A whole drag is one undo step, and Esc while dragging puts it back.
- **See what you will select.** With SELECT, whatever is under the mouse is outlined faintly before you click.

## Compatibility

No network protocol change (still 21): v0.15.2 plays with v0.15 and v0.14 clients and servers.
