# FERRITE v0.15.6

New music for Relay, Spillway and FORGE, a file picker for your player icon, and cleaner arrows in the launcher.

## Changes

- **Map scores for Relay and Spillway.** Relay has "Relay Static" and Spillway has "Spillway Hours", alongside Slag Cathedral's own score. A map's score plays in its lobby, and in its matches when MUSIC IN A MATCH is on, in place of the lobby track and the rotation. Going to a map with a different score fades the old one out first.
- **FORGE has its own track.** "Half Turn" plays on the FORGE start screen and in the editor, and loops for as long as you build. Leaving for the launcher brings the song rotation back; a playtest has the usual match music.
- **"Liquid Plasma" is launcher music.** It stays in the launcher's rotation but no longer comes up in a match with MUSIC IN A MATCH on. Submariner, Barbed Wire and your own songs still do.
- **Choose your player icon from a file.** Click the icon square on the home screen to pick a PNG in the system's file dialog (Windows; AppleScript's on macOS; zenity or kdialog on Linux). Dropping a PNG on the window still works. An empty square shows a "+".
- **Drawn arrows in the launcher.** The home page buttons end in a proper arrow, centred on the button and nudging right on hover, instead of a ">" sitting on the text line. Step buttons, BACK and NEXT, the map and gun pickers, the music skip button, the pause menu and FORGE's BACK card use the same drawn chevrons and arrows.

## Compatibility

No network protocol change (still 21): v0.15.6 plays with v0.15 and v0.14 clients and servers. Everything here is in the game client; servers do not need updating.
