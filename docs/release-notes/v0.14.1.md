# FERRITE v0.14.1

Sharper guns, shaped scenery and signs with some life in them, and every setting within reach during a match.

## New

- **HD weapons.** Every gun has about three times the detail. Parts are rounded rather than boxed, with a curved magazine on the Carbine and Lancer, stocks that deepen toward the butt, and grips that swell for the palm. Close up there are rail teeth, pins, ribbed grips and butt pads, slots in the handguards, and fine serrations, an ejection port and a slide stop on the pistols.
- **Every setting during a match.** SETTINGS in the pause menu now opens the launcher's full AUDIO / VIDEO / CONTROLS page, key bindings included, in place of the four sliders it had before. Changes take effect without leaving the match.
- **Cover with real shapes.** The props that fill every map have been rebuilt:
  - **Concrete:** low walls are Jersey barriers in sections, and blocks are chamfered and stained at the foot.
  - **Containers:** corrugated sides, corner posts and castings, and doors with locking bars and handles.
  - **Sandbags:** walls of rounded bags laid in staggered courses.
  - **Crates:** planks with gaps, cross braces and lids.
  - **Steel:** storage tanks with ladders and cone roofs, round chimneys, lamp posts, H-beams, corrugated roofs and plates with stiffeners.
- **Painted signs.** Building, tunnel and gantry names are enamel nameboards in each place's own colours with an emblem: a gear for Valley Works, a pickaxe for the stone works and mine, a pine for the Ranger Station, a chevron for Freight Control and Ferrite Freight, and an arch for the Service Tunnel and Transit 07. The lettering is smoothed and painted with a drop shadow, and each board hangs a touch off level under two gooseneck lamps. Warnings are yellow and black hazard plates, with BREACH in red.

## Fixes

- **The FPS line stays out of the way.** The F3 FPS and ping readout no longer runs through the text of the settings page.
- **The passage sign.** The WEST/EAST plate in the passage was half hidden inside the frame. It now stands on its own post.

All the new detail is scenery: what you collide with and can shoot through is unchanged.

## Compatibility

No network protocol change (still 21): v0.14.1 plays with v0.14.0 clients and servers.
