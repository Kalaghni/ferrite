# FERRITE v0.15.1

Fixes for maps made in FORGE, and trees you can now pick up and move.

## Fixes

- **Canopy remixes keep their fig.** A map copied from Canopy carried the fig's trunks, root walls and bough walkways, but they were invisible: you could walk into walls you could not see. They are drawn again, in bark with root ribs and plank walkways, and the leaf crown hangs over them as long as both trunks are still standing. The seedline cables are drawn too, so riders can see the lines.
- **Posts show on custom maps.** Wooden posts (the POST block, and the legs of the WATCHTOWER model) were invisible on maps made in FORGE. They are drawn as timber now.

## New

- **Select trees in FORGE.** With SELECT (1), click a tree or its mirrored twin. Arrows move it over the ground and PgUp/PgDn lift it, Shift+Up/Down make it taller or shorter, Shift+Left/Right widen or narrow its crown, R swaps pine and broadleaf, T changes its colour, Ctrl+D copies it and Delete removes it.

The undergrowth (grass, ferns, bushes) comes from the map's mood and is not something you can select; it has no collision.

## Compatibility

No network protocol change (still 21): v0.15.1 plays with v0.15.0 and v0.14 clients and servers.
