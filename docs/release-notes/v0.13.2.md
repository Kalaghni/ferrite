# FERRITE v0.13.2

## New

- **The volcano is heard.** The ground rumbles as Slag Cathedral's volcano builds up and while its lava fountain plays, and it blows with a deep boom and a long rolling echo that you hear, and feel a little, anywhere on the map.
- **Lava down the cone.** The overflow from the crater is now a molten river from the rim into the head of the runner. It has crusted banks, a white-hot core with crust drifting down it, heat haze, smoke and sparks. It pours down the cone in a few seconds as the eruption starts and crusts over with the runner.
- **Turn your soldier in the armory.** Click and drag to turn the soldier, or the gun in its panel, round to any side.

## Fixes

- **Killcams reach the kill.** When the wait to redeploy is short (team deathmatch, an early conquest wave), the killcam used to be cut off by the respawn before it got to the shot that killed you. It now starts sooner and goes back less far, so it always plays through the kill before you are back in.
- **The lobby header has room.** On larger screens the wordmark no longer runs into the key hints, and the map cards, mode chips and roster sit below it with space between them. The hints are drawn as keycaps over a dark band, so they read over any background.

## Compatibility

No network protocol change (still 20): v0.13.2 plays with v0.13.0 and v0.13.1 clients and servers.
