# FERRITE v0.15.0

Make your own maps. FORGE, the map maker, is on the launcher: build in 3D, shape the ground, stamp models from a library, and playtest against bots in a keypress. An assistant such as Claude can work on the map beside you through the new MCP server. Spawns are reworked too, so the enemy no longer knows where you will come from.

## New

- **FORGE, the map maker.** MAP MAKER on the launcher (or `ferrite --forge`) opens a start screen with a card for every map you have made, newest first, each with a top-down plan, when it was last edited and how many blocks it has.
  - **New maps** start blank, on the ground of any built-in map, or as a copy of a built-in map to remix (not yet for Quarry and Ridgeline, whose tunnels the editor cannot carry). Copies are folded to their west half, so every edit lands on both sides.
  - **Maps are files.** Each map is one `.fmap` in the `maps` folder beside the game. OPEN FOLDER shows it, and maps copied in by hand appear on the start screen by themselves, so sharing a map is passing a file around.
  - **Unsaved work is kept.** FORGE autosaves every minute. A map with unsaved work is badged UNSAVED WORK, and opening it offers to restore that work or open the last save. Leaving with unsaved changes asks once, then keeps them.
- **Building.** Fly with WASD and Q/E while holding the right mouse button. Every edit is mirrored by a half turn, so both teams get the same map.
  - **Place** eight kinds of block, with a ghost of the block and its turned twin; R turns, T tints.
  - **Select** to move (arrow keys, PgUp/PgDn), resize (Shift), turn, retint, duplicate (Ctrl+D) or delete.
  - **Objectives and bases** are placed with their own tool.
  - **Undo and redo** with Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y. Ctrl+S saves, Ctrl+Shift+S saves as a new map, and Esc goes back to the start screen.
  - **F5 playtests** the map with bots; leaving the match returns you to the editor.
- **The library.** L opens a drawer of models in folders: a built-in set of blocks, cover, structures and nature, plus your own. Drag a card into the world to place it (R turns it), or click it to stamp it with every click. Your models live in the `prefabs` folder beside the game, and new ones appear in the drawer by themselves.
- **Terrain.** The TERRAIN tool (key 4) shapes the ground with a round brush: RAISE, LOWER, FLATTEN, SMOOTH, RAMP and NOISE. Hold and drag on the ground (for RAMP, drag from the foot to the top). Tab changes the mode, [ and ] the size, - and = the strength. A whole stroke is one undo step, and Esc takes it back while you are still dragging. Trees, blocks standing on the ground and anything stacked on them move with it; blocks in the air stay where they are.
- **The FORGE MCP server.** `ferrite-forge-mcp` ships beside the game and lets an assistant such as Claude build with you. When FORGE has the map open its edits appear on your screen as they happen, each batch one undo step; otherwise it works on the file. It can:
  - list, create and describe maps
  - place, move, restyle and remove blocks, sculpt the ground, and stamp or save library models
  - draw a top-down plan, take screenshots and point your camera at something
  - check the map: routes, cover and sight lines at each objective, spawn exposure, mirror pairs, floating or buried blocks, and the ground under each objective
  - run a two-minute bot match in about a second and report where the fighting happened

  To add it to Claude Code: `claude mcp add ferrite-forge -- <path to ferrite-forge-mcp>`.
- **Spawns.** A team no longer comes in at one spot. It has spawn zones along its own stretch of the front line: its base, the points it holds and the ground between them. Each arrival is placed out of the enemy's sight, away from where teammates just appeared and not on top of anyone, with a little chance thrown in. Holding more ground gives your team more places to come from, and there is no single doorway for the other side to watch.

## Limits

- Custom maps are played on your own machine against bots: F5 in FORGE, or `ferrite --host --map-file FILE`. Other players cannot join a custom map yet; dedicated servers and sending maps to other players come later.
- The ground has a single colour palette, there are no water or lava areas yet, and its heights sit on a 2 m grid. Players and bots can walk up any slope, so a cliff that must stop people should be rock blocks.
- Trees, ladders and seedlines cannot be placed in the editor yet.

## Compatibility

No network protocol change (still 21): v0.15.0 plays with v0.14 clients and servers. The new spawns come with the server, so they apply on any server running v0.15.0, whatever version the players have.
