# FERRITE v0.9.2

## Bigger battlefields

- Every map is spread out by half again, to 354 by 204 metres. Neighbouring objectives are 59 to 98 metres apart instead of 39 to 66, and the bases 318 metres apart instead of 212. Each map's hills, pit, ridge, channel or plateau grew with it. Buildings keep their size and their place beside the objective they belong to.
- The new ground holds buildings with doors and windows, open sheds, tanks, stair-climbed lookouts and walled ruins, themed to each map: farms and workshops in Foundry Valley, site offices and machine sheds in Quarry, cabins and hunters' lookouts on Ridgeline, warehouses in Depot, pump houses and settling tanks on Spillway, equipment huts on Relay, and ingot stores and gas holders round Slag Cathedral.
- On Relay the array towers, dishes and cable gantries moved out with their objectives; the exchange towers beside C stayed put. On Slag Cathedral the cathedral, galleries, furnaces and lava stayed put while the yards and bases moved out. See the [Relay](../maps/relay.md) and [Slag Cathedral](../maps/slag-cathedral.md) guides.
- The minimap stays the same size on screen.

## Spawns

- Players deploy anywhere on open ground up to 20 metres from the centre of their base, or 6 to 16 metres from a point their team holds. The game picks the open spot farthest from other players, and never one in lava.

## Launcher and controls

- Team is no longer chosen in the launcher. Everyone joins on auto-balance and picks a side in the lobby with the change-team key (M by default).
- The player icon picker sits beside your callsign.
- The sight you choose for each weapon is remembered between matches.
- The number keys (class, primary, sidearm, look and finishes) change your loadout only while the loadout panel is up: in the lobby, while waiting to redeploy, or with the panel opened. Mid-fight they no longer swap your next deployment by accident.
- F11 toggles fullscreen in the launcher and in a match, and the choice is saved like the setting in SETTINGS.

## Fixes

- Standing in lava now shows the red damage overlay, pulsing for as long as you burn. Before, lava damage was silent on screen.
- Surfaces no longer flicker between two shades as you look around on Slag Cathedral. Overlapping blocks that share a face now take one shade, and the dark corner posts stand just proud of the walls instead of flush with them.
- The Windows build no longer prints two compiler warnings from the bundled window library.

## Compatibility

Collision changed on every map and the network protocol is now 15. Everyone in a match needs v0.9.2, including the dedicated server. Players on v0.9.1 can use **UPDATE & RESTART** in the launcher.

## Verification

- A fingerprint test pins the layout of all seven maps. Map checks cover how far apart objectives are, the buildings between them, open spawn ground and navigation. All workspace tests pass.
- Not yet verified in play: the lava overlay, sight memory across matches, F11, and the flicker fix on Slag Cathedral.
