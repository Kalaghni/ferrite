# FERRITE v0.11.0

This release also brings everything from v0.10.0, which was not released on its own: four game modes (Conquest, Team Deathmatch, Capture the Flag, Stronghold), kill streaks and pickups, credits and the ARMORY, a first-launch introduction, foliage and weather on the classic maps, and touch controls. See the [v0.10.0 release notes](v0.10.0.md).

## Landmarks

The four oldest battlefields have authored places to fight through, and every map has a silhouette to navigate by. Playable additions are mirrored under a half turn, so both teams get the same routes and cover.

| Map | New |
| --- | --- |
| Foundry Valley | Boiler workshops with four doors, windows and work benches, a covered service culvert, twin stacks with drifting exhaust and a working flywheel |
| Quarry | Site workshops and long timbered mine adits with rails and two exits, under an 18-metre headframe with turning winding wheels and moving skips |
| Ridgeline | Ranger lodges, rock passages, wind turbines and the **Fallen Giant**: a crashed cargo aircraft across the central saddle, with C on the floor of its walkable hold |
| Depot | Dispatch warehouses and open-ended container passages under a 52-metre gantry with a travelling trolley and a hanging spreader |
| Relay | A giant cellular mast beyond the boundary, with antenna crowns and aviation lights |
| Slag Cathedral | A distant volcano that sometimes erupts. It sits beyond the boundary and does no damage |

### The Fallen Giant

The cargo hold runs along the saddle. Both broken ends are open and three tears in each side let you in without jumping or crouching. The roof and intact hull are real cover; loose skin panels rattle in the wind and an emergency beacon still flashes inside. See the [Fallen Giant guide](../maps/fallen-giant.md).

### Explosive changes

Wall panels, service walkways and machine controls marked with hazard paint respond to a grenade going off close by, though not to gunfire:

- a **breached panel** opens a new way through;
- a **collapsed walkway** drops its deck, and whoever was on it falls;
- a **disabled control** stops its machine.

The server decides each one, updates collision and bot navigation, and sends the lasting state in every snapshot, so a late joiner sees the same map. A new round restores everything. See the [expanded map guide](../maps/landmarks.md).

## Soldiers

Soldiers are rebuilt to match the launcher art: heavy, broad-shouldered troopers made of faceted plates, pale armour over a dark undersuit, a domed helmet with a glowing visor, big shoulder plates, wrap-round greaves and a pack whose lid carries the team colour and two pale bars. Team colour is on the shoulders, the pack, a chest tab and the visor, so sides still read at a distance. All twelve looks are rebuilt in the same style; Scout, Veteran and Hazard show proper faces.

## Twice the detail

Every map has about twice the polygons of v0.10:

- the ground is drawn on a grid twice as fine, rounding off slopes while staying within 5 cm of the ground you walk on;
- every tube and turned shape (barrels, pipes, dishes, bells, limbs) is cut twice as fine;
- trees have rounder crowns, drooping pine tiers and eight-sided trunks; bushes are rounder; grass blades bend.

On a desktop GPU the frame rate is unchanged.

## Fixes

- **Bots no longer see through trees.** Leaves now hide a soldier from a bot as a wall would, unless that soldier fires where the bot is looking. Bullets still pass through leaves.
- **Capture the Flag:** while carrying the enemy flag, your own flag's DEFEND marker no longer sits on top of BRING IT HERE.
- **Hosting:** a hosted server is ready the moment the game starts joining it.

## Compatibility

The network protocol changed (18) and every map's collision changed, so everyone in a match needs v0.11.0, including the dedicated server.
