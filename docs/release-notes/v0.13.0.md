# FERRITE v0.13.0

Three maps now change while you fight on them. The volcano, the dam and the great mast all run on the shared world clock, so every player - including anyone who joins late - sees the same eruption or flood at the same moment.

## Slag Cathedral - the lava runner

A stone-lined runner carries lava from the volcano onto the field, on the centre line and the same distance from both bases: across the south yard, under the service passage, through the nave's south door and into the southern casting channel. It is 3.8 m wide - more than a comfortable jump - and dry most of the time.

- When the volcano erupts (in about two windows out of five), the crater breaches and lava pours down the cone into the runner. It reaches the casting channel about 17 seconds after it appears at the fence.
- Each stretch stays molten for 20 to 35 seconds, then the whole runner crusts over and is safe again, glowing and cracking as it cools.
- While it flows, the south side is cut in two. Cross on the steel gratings in the south yard and under the service passage, over the gallery inside the nave, or go round the north side. It burns like the casting channels (40 health a second).
- Bots wait at the edge and carry on once it has crusted.

See the [Slag Cathedral guide](../maps/slag-cathedral.md#the-lava-runner).

## Spillway - the gate deck and the flood

C no longer sits on the channel floor. It stands on the old floodgate's deck, raised over the channel on piers, with railed bridges to both banks, stairs down to the channel either side, mirrored cover, and the lifted gate hanging overhead.

- After every siren test at the Great Dam, the outlet gates in both banks lift. Water bursts out at C, runs both ways along the channel, rises to 1.9 m and drains away about 45 seconds later, leaving wet ground and puddles. The dam's middle gate lifts and the reservoir pours down the chute while the release lasts.
- Water deeper than 0.3 m does 30 damage a second, so a full channel kills in a little over three seconds. The deck, the bridges, the stairs above the water and the shallows at the edges are safe.
- C counts only players on the deck, not anyone in the channel underneath.
- Bots take the bridges and stairs, avoid the channel while the sirens sound or water is out, and climb out if they are caught in it.

See the [Spillway guide](../maps/spillway.md).

## Relay - BACKBONE, the great mast

The 67 m cellular mast has moved from beyond the ridge onto the field: on the north flank, on the centre line, the same distance from both spawns. Its legs, footings, decks, rails, panels and equipment hut are all solid.

- **Ladders.** Climb the caged ladder to a rest platform at 20 m (with a waist-high parapet for cover), then a second ladder to the top deck at 36.75 m.
- **Climbing.** Look at the rungs and push forward to take hold; forward climbs, back climbs down, crouch slides down fast, jump lets go. At the top you step off onto the deck; to go down, walk backwards into the hatch.
- **Risk.** It takes about 12 seconds to reach the top, and you cannot shoot, throw or melee while on a ladder, with your back to the field.
- Bots stay on the ground and route round the legs.

See the [Relay guide](../maps/relay.md#backbone-the-great-mast).
