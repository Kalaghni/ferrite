# Future map landmarks

Saved from the user's ideas on November 15, 2026. All four concepts are now built. Ridgeline's Fallen Giant is a playable landmark; see its [map guide](fallen-giant.md). The Broken Viaduct, Titan Excavator and Great Dam are scenery-only monuments; see [Monuments](landmarks.md#monuments) for where they stand and how they are reviewed. The original concepts are preserved below.

The shared direction is monumental landmarks that dwarf the soldiers, give each map a distinctive silhouette, and feel alive through small ambient movements and sounds.

## Foundry Valley - The Broken Viaduct

**Status:** Implemented as scenery. A steel lattice arch carries the line 44 m over the battlefield along x = 0, from ridge to ridge; the north approach span has fallen onto the slope beyond the fence and the engine hangs over the gap.

A colossal railway bridge spanning the entire valley. One section has collapsed, leaving a freight locomotive hanging over the gap. Rusted steel trusses, broken tracks, and abandoned ore cars complete the scene.

**Ambient touch:** The train's remaining carriage occasionally creaks in the wind. A dangling signal lamp still blinks.

## Quarry - The Titan Excavator

**Status:** Implemented as scenery. The dragline stands on the south ridge beyond the fence; its 172 m boom reaches over the terraces and the bucket hangs about 33 m above the pit floor north of C.

A ridiculously oversized walking dragline excavator. Its boom stretches across much of the quarry, with a bucket large enough to swallow a truck. The machine dwarfs the soldiers and terraced pit walls.

**Ambient touch:** The bucket sways, cables groan, and the giant mechanism occasionally rotates a few degrees.

## Ridgeline - The Fallen Giant

**Status:** Implemented in the current development build. The original concept is preserved below.

A massive crashed military cargo aircraft embedded in the ridge. Its fuselage rests across a rocky pass, with one wing buried in the forest and the tail towering over the treetops.

**Ambient touch:** A surviving emergency beacon flashes inside the wreck. Loose metal panels rattle in the mountain wind.

## Spillway - The Great Dam

**Status:** Implemented as scenery. A 66 m gravity dam closes the valley beyond the north fence, between rock shoulders, with five floodgates under a hoist bridge and powerhouses at its toe.

An immense hydroelectric dam towering over the drained waterworks. Huge concrete retaining walls, giant steel floodgates, and a maintenance gantry suspended high overhead.

**Ambient touch:** Warning sirens occasionally test, old machinery hums, and water trickles through the supposedly sealed gates.
